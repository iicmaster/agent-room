## Findings

**F1 · The workflow returned to service and survived its guards — but not the provider.**
Story 3.4 ran through the workflow: plan, implement, four internal reviews, and the coordinator
completed unattended; the guards fired CORRECTLY twice (scope on the upkeep set; fail-closed on an
in-flight outbox) and both produced permanent fixes (the standard-upkeep allowlist; race
documentation). The triage phase then died twice on provider errors (`Model creation failed`) and
the patch+finalize was hand-run. *Sources: `dwfrun-{a76f2e44,b2e47213,b65af21e,6c7e9803}*`,
commits `a4267d9`, `642bb48`, `2620c53`.* *Disposition: accept — phases 1–6 are now proven
unattended; phases 8–10 await a stable provider window. The proof marker was deliberately not
written (it must record a FULLY unattended close).*

**F2 · The remote trust lane needed a seam, and the seam is the Epic 4 input.**
Stories 3.2–3.4 could prove holds only at the composed layer (Local Mode auto-delivers). Story 3.5
landed a minimal fail-closed declaration (`AGENT_ROOM_CONNECTION_MODE=remote`): it can only
withhold what Local Mode would deliver, never grant; 'local' stays earned. *Source:
`server/client/api-client.mjs` (declaredConnectionMode), `fe2913b`.* *Disposition: accept — Epic
4's challenge-authenticated client makes this the natural path; first production exercise of the
remote lane happens there.*

**F3 · The reviewers' sharpest finding was a UX-dead-end, and it was real.**
`allow <name> --remove` was unusable once the sender went offline past presence's 24 h window —
the standing grant had no human revocation path. Fixed: removal resolves from the stored
allowlist. *Source: internal-1-1 (medium), fixed in `c62b8c6`.* *Disposition: fixed.*

**F4 · Honesty surfaces are now grep-guarded.**
The envelope label's Instructed framing (3.1), the CLI help's "approval is a human convention"
wording and the absence of any "never agent-invocable" claim (3.4) are all pinned by tests — a
future edit that overclaims fails the suite. *Sources: `tests/mcp-tools.test.mjs` (3.1 section),
`tests/cli-surface.test.mjs`.* *Disposition: accept.*

## Open questions

- **The declared-remote seam (`AGENT_ROOM_CONNECTION_MODE`)** is an environment declaration, not a
  cryptographic fact. Epic 4's challenge-response authentication replaces it with a proven mode;
  until then, a hostile local process could declare remote to force holds on itself (self-denial,
  not privilege gain) — fail-closed by design.
- **Accept-all remains unreachable from any tool** by design; its only setter is the human CLI
  (3.4). Whether it ever needs an agent-mediated path is a product question deferred to real use.
