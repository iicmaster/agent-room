## Intent

**Problem:** `docs/epics.md:580-589` — Story 6.2, REL-3, the formal soak. Every endurance claim so far is bounded: Story 2.4 proved the harness and a paced window, Story 2.5 proved continuity across kills, Story 4.8 proved remote churn — none proves the integrated system survives the SHAPE of a real day (hours of steady traffic, bursts, maximum payloads, clients churning) without leaking. The candidate is frozen (Story 6.3); the advisor consensus makes this story the second gate: the frozen RC must be soaked BEFORE the human's physical demo, so "the engine is bulletproof" is known before REL-1 spends human time.

**Approach:** one standalone long-runner, `tests/soak/rel3-soak.mjs` (NOT a node:test file — a detached 8-hour process cannot live inside the suite), plus a compressed-validation test that proves the harness mechanics at 5-minute scale. The runner: (1) materializes the RC and proves its identity under the candidate-immutability law (frozen-digest compare, git runtime-identity proof on drift, refuse before any spawn on a runtime change); (2) spawns the RC's own server child (four gates raised by the operator-env precedent) plus four concurrent MCP children of the SAME artifact (distinct keypairs, configured Chat IDs, converging DM ring — 5 rooms total); (3) runs the traffic profile — one message per room every 10 s ±20% jitter with senders rotating among the room's members, bursts every 10 minutes (duration/48, clamped, when compressed), payloads ramping linearly to the artifact's own `MESSAGE_TEXT_MAX` (32 KiB as ASCII, imported from the materialized constants module, never a local literal) across the first half and holding at the cap for the second; (4) reconnects every client hourly (duration/8 compressed) through the shipped session machinery — children drop, the harness's throwaway local exchanges evict the dropped tokens via the session registry's oldest-eviction capacity discipline, and each fresh child re-authenticates and re-binds the same Chat ID through the shipped re-auth recovery path (dead-holder release + re-claim, asserted); (5) samples the server child's RSS and FD count every 60 s (duration/40, clamped) through the Story 2.4 harness's external samplers, journals every acknowledged `(room_id, seq, client_message_id)` to the external REL-2 recorder process, and records the REL-4 idle-footprint samples (RSS/FD/cumulative-CPU with 0 Actors at start, in every reconnect window, and at the end; post-resume settled samples with the run's 4 Actors) — reported, never gated; (6) at the end: quiesce in-flight sends (resolve-to-outcome, never strand a committed-but-unacknowledged row), drain and byte-verify every room through the real tool surface (contiguity from 1, acked == readable, zero duplicates, foreign rows unwrapped from the literal v1 envelope), re-digest the artifact (unchanged by the run), and evaluate THE SINGLE PASS RULE. The warmup drives unpaced rounds AT THE PAYLOAD CAP until the trailing RSS slope flattens before the tracked window opens — the warm-then-measure discipline applied to this profile's heaviest demand, so the working-set growth the ramp drives happens before measurement, never inside it.

**The pass rule (verbatim, total-growth reading):** RSS least-squares slope over the FINAL FOUR HOURS ≤ 2% of starting RSS — implemented exactly as `(endRSS − startRSS_of_window) / RSS_at_window_start ≤ 0.02`, evaluated on the endpoint samples AND on the least-squares regression's implied growth across the window; the final four hours are the final HALF of the wall duration (exactly four hours at the default 8). FDs stable after hour one (every sample at or after duration/8 within ±5 of the hour-one baseline — the four-client reconnect drop is the worst transient at −4 and stays inside the corridor by design). Zero message loss as above. All three, plus artifact stability and zero loop errors, gate the verdict.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:** run the MATERIALIZED artifact's own entrypoints (server and MCP children resolve through the release dir; the payload cap imports from the artifact's constants module); prove the candidate identity BEFORE any spawn and refuse loudly on a runtime change; keep the whole run world in a 0700 mkdtemp parent outside the repository, the ack log at 0600 owned by the recorder process; derive every compressed schedule from the requested duration so the validation exercises the same mechanics at the same rule shapes (the steady 10 s per-room cadence is the profile's own and is never compressed); checkpoint the results JSON/MD with status 'running' every sampling interval so a crashed run leaves its numbers; warm the server child with an unpaced burst until the trailing RSS slope flattens BEFORE the tracked window opens (the Story 2.4 warm-then-measure discipline — a cold allocator's ramp must never masquerade as a leak); retry sends outcome-unknown with the IDENTICAL client_message_id under a 120 s resolve deadline; tear down in dependency order (traffic, clients, recorder, server) on every exit path, and keep the world on failure for autopsy.

**Block If:** the identity proof refuses (a packaged runtime input changed since the candidate commit — the candidate is void; cut a new one, rerun Story 6.3, restart the soak); the server child exits mid-run; any send fails to resolve within its deadline; traffic loops surface an error; the final drain finds a gap, a phantom, a duplicate, or a payload that does not read back byte-exact; the FD corridor breaks after hour one; the final-window total growth exceeds 2% of the window's starting RSS; the artifact re-digest at the end differs from the start.

**Never:** never run the repository tree as the system under test; never compress the per-room cadence or weaken a rule bound for the short run (the windows scale, the bounds never do); never gate on the REL-4 counter-metric or on the reported-not-gated 2.4 rate; never accept an `actor_id` from anywhere but the installation's own keypair/config (the NFR-3 posture holds inside the soak); never write into the repository from the compressed-validation test (its evidence goes to a temporary parent); never let an unhandled rejection kill the runner before results land (the tracker's sampler can legitimately throw at a vanished pid on a failing path — it is recorded as the fatal it is).
