## What worked (keep doing)

1. **The gate discipline paid for itself three times.** The 7.1 design gate
   refused the first A7 mechanism (both vendors, same counterexample: the
   send route awaits its body inside the stage loop, so a post-loop pin
   misses it); the 7.2 design gate caught that the drafted "plain bind =
   observation" was factually wrong (a free name CLAIMS — Sally/agy's
   free-name amendment) and settled the pair-vs-generation question; the
   7.3 review round executed probes that killed `process.once` and the
   missing total budget. Every refusal produced a committed, gated test.
2. **The recorded refusal as a deliverable (7.4)** closed A2 without
   building anything — and the refusal is STRONGER evidence than a fragile
   mechanism would have been (the ECONNREFUSED premise refuted from primary
   sources, not vibes).
3. **A1 held for the whole epic**: every packaged doc change landed with its
   story (CHANGELOG entries, README sections, the Spine amendments, the CLI
   help). No post-freeze text churn.
4. **Evidence-never-edited discipline**: 7.6's disposition recomputed from
   the archived trace and DISAGREED with the recorded diagnostic — the
   resolution was a disposition record + a pinned recomputation beside the
   untouched evidence, not a touch of the captured numbers.
